EasyTier FFI overlay for core/Clash.Meta, plus Tailscale Android hardenings.

Base: chen08209/Clash.Meta @ 70f0570405c3c2c47bb113b88db95006d239b346 (FlClash)
Source: https://github.com/g3237582/mihomo/tree/cursor/easytier-outbound-d1fd
PR: https://github.com/g3237582/mihomo/pull/1

Also overlays:
- adapter/outbound/tailscale.go (wait until IPN Running or TailscaleIPs, empty
  bind check, panic recover)
- adapter/outbound/tailscale_harden.go
- component/iface/anet/anet.go (IsForceAnet always true on Android)
- component/iface/anet/anet_fallback.go (netlink preferred, stdlib fallback)
- component/iface/anet/interface_android.go (FORCE_ANET is preference, not sole path)

Android libclash.so additionally sets FORCE_ANET=1 in core/lib.go as the
preferred interface source. Clash.Meta/main.go DefaultResolver os.Exit is not
linked on Android.

Applied by tool/apply_easytier_overlay.sh and by the setup Go hook.
